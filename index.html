Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker

Effective date: 14 September 2026

This policy explains how the Expense Tracker Android app ("the app"), developed by Alan Sangeeth S ("I", "me"), handles your information. The app is designed to work entirely on your device.

  • All your data is stored only on your device.
  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere.
  • No accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • SMS messages are read on the device only to detect bank debit alerts, and the message text is never saved.
  • I never collect, sell or share your data.

1. Information the app handles

The app handles the following information only on your device:

  • Financial records you enter: transactions (amount, date, category, account and note), categories, accounts with opening balances, monthly budgets and currency preference.
  • Custom SMS keywords you add: words you set up to match banks, categories, accounts and debit phrases.
  • App preferences: the last period and filters you selected.
  • Incoming SMS messages: read only as described in section 2, if you grant the permission.

None of this information is sent to me or to any third party.

2. SMS permission (RECEIVE_SMS)

The app requests the RECEIVE_SMS permission so it can detect bank transaction alerts and help you record spending without typing it. This feature is optional. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

When a new SMS arrives, the app:

  • Checks, on your device, whether the message is a debit alert from a supported bank or a bank you added.
  • Ignores all other messages, including personal messages, OTPs, credits, reminders and promotions. They are not saved or processed further.
  • For a debit alert, extracts the amount, date, merchant name, and a suggested account and category, and shows you a notification.
  • Saves nothing until you tap the notification, review the prefilled record and choose to save it.

The full SMS text is never stored, uploaded or shared. The app only reacts to new messages as they arrive and does not read your existing SMS inbox.

3. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

The app uses the notification permission only to show a prompt when a bank debit alert is detected. Notifications are created on your device and contain the detected amount, account, category and merchant.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access. The app does not include any internet access, analytics, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

5. Backups

  • Manual backup and import: you can export your data to a JSON file and import it later. The file is saved only where you choose with the Android file picker. If you save it to a cloud service, that service's privacy policy applies.
  • Android system backup: if Google backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. This is controlled by Android and Google, not by the app. You can turn it off in Android Settings.

6. Sharing of information

I do not collect, sell, rent, trade or share any personal or financial information. The app is distributed through Google Play, and Google may collect information under its own privacy policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual records, categories and accounts in the app.
  • Use Delete all records in the app menu to clear all transactions.
  • Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete all app data from your device.

Because I never receive your data, I have no copy to delete.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
